--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} A documentation page that arrives without a heading gets the one its front matter names. The sequencer repo's scripts/build_site_docs.py strips the source H1 on the way out, so the page title is single-sourced from its site-manifest.yml and cannot drift from the breadcrumb and the nav. What it does not do is put the title back, and nothing else here ever did: page.title reaches and stops. Without this, 58 of the 59 generated pages render with no heading at all, opening mid-prose under the breadcrumb. Scoped by URL rather than given a layout of its own, so the generator carries on emitting `layout: page` and this site stays independent of it. about.md and the other hand-written pages share this layout and open with their own `##`, so the URL test leaves them alone. The `<h1` test is what makes this safe to land before the pages are regenerated: the copies presently in the repo still carry their source heading, and supplying a second one would give them two. It reads as the rule it is — a page gets exactly one heading, from whichever source has it. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if page.url contains '/documentation/MSEQ' -%} {%- unless content contains '<h1' -%} <h1>{{ page.title | escape }}</h1> {%- endunless -%} {%- endif -%} {{ content }}
