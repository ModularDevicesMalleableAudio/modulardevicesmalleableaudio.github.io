{%- comment -%} Browser-tab title. `page.title` alone gave the 57 generated MSEQ pages titles like "Swing" and "Trigs": indistinguishable in a tab strip, a bookmark list or a search result, and with nothing to say they belong to MSEQ, to the documentation, or to this site. The suffix supplies that context, most-specific first, so a truncated tab still shows the page's own name. Pages whose own title already says MSEQ — the manual, the contents page — skip that segment rather than stutter it. The suffix is chosen here from the URL rather than set in each page's front matter, because the MSEQ pages are build output from the sequencer repo's scripts/build_site_docs.py and carry only layout, title and permalink.
